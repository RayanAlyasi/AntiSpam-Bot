## 2026-10-17 · F-065 · Serial executor per bot with SKIP LOCKED and crash recovery (WP-H)

- Built: on Postgres, `PGStore.Lock` holds a session advisory lock on a dedicated connection for
  as long as the executor runs, so exactly one executor serves a bot across processes and a crashed
  process releases it; `Claim` uses `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`; `Recover` returns actions left running
  to pending on start; the candidate window (per priority and chat, plus newest messages and
  earliest deadlines) is refilled from the table, which stays the source of truth.
- The executor itself shipped in #22; this adds the persistent store under it.
- Contract: `.agent/contracts/F-064.md`.
