## 2026-10-17 · F-074 · Circuit breaker and global kill switch (WP-H)

- Built (#22, #31): the breaker opens on a temporary ban from any call, on 3 warning signs within
  10 minutes, or through `Executor.OpenBreaker`; while open only P0 lockdowns run, the rest stays
  queued (detections continue), and the operator is alerted; it closes only through
  `Operator.CloseBreaker`. `Operator.Pause` is the global kill switch. Both persist in
  `app.action_controls`.
- This PR wires it: `actions.Runner` implements `alert.Breaker`, so the connector's health
  tracker trips it on `TemporaryBan`, `LoggedOut` and `StreamReplaced` (also for a bot whose
  executor runs in another process: the open breaker is persisted), and breaker alerts reach the
  alert sinks as critical.
- Contract: `.agent/contracts/F-064.md`.
