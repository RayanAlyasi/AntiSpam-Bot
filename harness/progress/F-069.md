## 2026-10-17 · F-069 · Slow-down on warning signs, recovery capped at the ceiling (WP-H)

- Built (#22, #31): a 429, an unusual error, a call slower than 10 s, or a disconnect within a
  minute of a write cuts the rate by `pacing.slowdown_factor` (0.25, at least 2/min); it recovers
  `recovery_step_per_min` per `recovery_interval` of calm, never above the ceiling; the slowed rate
  is persisted; `Operator.SetCeiling` is audited in the same transaction; a daily writes alert
  fires once per UTC day past `daily_alert_writes`.
- This PR wires it into the binary: `actions.Runner` runs one executor per connected bot in the
  connector role, connection events feed the disconnect warning, and queue alerts go to the
  operator's alert sinks (`cmd/antispam/actions.go`).
- Contract: `.agent/contracts/F-064.md`.

### Follow-ups
- The operator page (raise the ceiling, pause, close the breaker) belongs to the dashboard
  features; `actions.Operator` is the API it calls.
