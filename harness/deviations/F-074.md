## 2026-10-17 · F-074 · §10.5

- **The breaker closes only by operator action.** `alert.Breaker.Trip` passes when a temporary ban
  lifts (`until`), and its doc says writes stop "until the given time"; the queue only logs that
  time and keeps the breaker open until an operator closes it. A ban is evidence that the number's
  behaviour should be checked before writing again, so the conservative reading wins.
- **Disconnect warning signs are counted once per outage.** whatsmeow reports a keepalive timeout
  every 30–40 s until it reconnects; counting each one would open the breaker after a single
  network blip.
