## 2026-10-17 · F-045 · Language and script anomaly signals (T1)

Part of the WP-G bundle; see `harness/progress/F-041.md` for the bundle, its follow-ups and `harness/deviations/F-041.md` for deviations.

- Built: `script.MixedWords` flags words of 3+ letters mixing Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Armenian or Cherokee; Arabic or other scripts mixed with Latin don't count. The T1 analyzer emits `text.mixed_script` (+1.5, +0.5 per extra word, max 3) and `text.invisible_chars` from `Stripped` (+0.5 at 3, +1.0 at 10, +1.5 at 30).
- Deferred: A "script not used by the group's languages" signal was left out: it would fire on expats' ordinary messages.
- Follow-ups: see F-041.md.
