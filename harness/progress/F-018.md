## 2026-10-17 · F-018 · group state from events plus a 6 h refresh (WP-F, PR 2)

- Built: `app.groups` (migration 00009) and `identity.NewPostgresGroupStore`. `identity.Tracker` keeps name, topic, size, addressing mode, lock/announce/approval flags and bot-is-admin (and so the status: pending_admin, active, left) current from `JoinedGroup` and `GroupInfo` events. The connector role runs `Tracker.SyncJoined` per bot right after it connects and every 6 h: one `JoinedGroups` IQ refreshes every group, seeds groups the bot was added to while offline (their members pre-existing) and marks groups the bot is no longer in as left.
- Tests: `TestGroupEvents`, `TestJoinedGroupSeedsPreExistingMembers`, `TestRefreshPrunesAndDoesNotMarkNewcomersPreExisting`, `TestRefreshStaleMarksVanishedGroupsLeft`, `TestSyncJoined`, `TestPostgresGroupStore` (integration).
