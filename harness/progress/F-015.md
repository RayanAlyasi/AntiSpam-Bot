## 2026-10-17 · F-015 · inbox worker, NOTIFY wake-up and 72 h retention (WP-F, PR 2)

- Built: `inbox.Worker` claims rows with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` and a claim lease (`claimed_until`), so any number of workers in any number of processes share rows and a crashed worker's rows come back when the claim expires; an insert trigger NOTIFYs `app_inbox` and the workers LISTEN (a 5 s poll covers a lost connection). Failed rows retry with backoff, give up after 5 attempts or on `ErrPermanent`, and record the error. The hourly River job `inbox_retention` deletes inbox rows older than 72 h and expired message_index rows. `inbox.Dispatcher` is the handler wired in the workers role.
- Tests: `TestWorkerProcessesRetriesAndGivesUp`, `TestCrashedClaimIsResumed`, `TestWorkersShareRowsAndWakeOnNotify`, `TestPurgeAndMessageIndex` (integration), `TestDispatcherRoutesToSinks`.
- A shutdown hands a claimed batch back without spending attempts.
- Follow-ups: an `ObserveSender` error blocks routing (retried, then given up after 5 attempts); a Commands-sink failure re-runs the Moderate sink on retry (the action queue's dedupe keys absorb duplicates); NOTIFY also fires for duplicate inserts. Rows of one chat may be processed out of order across workers (an edit before its original); the pipeline should look the original up in message_index and tolerate a miss.
