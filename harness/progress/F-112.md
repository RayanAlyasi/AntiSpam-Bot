# F-112 · Early-AI safety: ai evidence never auto-bans without a track record

## 2026-10-17

Built in the WP-J bundle (log: `harness/progress/F-106.md`): `internal/ai/safety`. `Decide` strips `ai` from every matrix cell's `require_source` unless the track record is met (`TrackRecord`; `Unmet{}` in v0.1), never auto-bans an ai finding directly or through a review timeout, and leaves known evidence untouched. `AllowAISource` answers whether `.autoban … ai` may be accepted. Property tests cover every preset, mode, content type, standing and cell.

## Follow-ups
- wp-i's `policy.Decide` still honours `ai` in `require_source` by itself; callers must use `safety.Decide` (wp-l told). Moving the check into `internal/policy` would close that gap for good.
- Track-record tracking itself lands in v0.4.
