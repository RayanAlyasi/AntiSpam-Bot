## 2026-10-17 · F-077 · local ban = kick + rejoin block (WP-I, fourth PR)

- Built: `policy.Bans` (`Ban` records group_bans with reason spam/other/policy and the decider, and kicks at P2; `OnJoin` kicks a banned identity that rejoins at P0, or lifts the ban when a current admin deliberately re-added them; `Unban` lifts the block and never re-adds), its Postgres store (`store.PolicyStore`, migration 00006, PR #27), and `policy.QueueKicker`, the adapter that enqueues the removal on wp-h's action queue for the group's bot (coalesced kicks, dedupe per ban and per rejoin; a ban whose earlier kick failed, expired, was cancelled or refused kicks again under a derived retry key).
- Deviations: harness/deviations/F-077.md (admin re-add lifts a local ban).

- Follow-ups:
