# F-076 deviations (safety rails)

- §8.6 "beyond that the bot pauses and alerts": the deletion circuit stays open until an admin or the operator resets it (`Rails.ResetCircuit`); it does not close on its own. While open, decisions are recorded as shadow and nothing is enqueued.
- Bans over the hourly cap become review actions with a shared `ReviewRequest.Batch` key per group and cap window; the review layer (wp-k) merges requests with the same key into one "kick the remaining N?" item.
- Per-rule kill switches are applied by `Rails.FilterFindingFor` (through `Engine.Route`) before Decide: killed signals and violations are removed, the score is recomputed without the killed weights, and where the killed items were the finding's evidence (its only known evidence, all its violations, or any of its heuristic signals) the band becomes still uncertain if real evidence (not a prior) remains and the new score still reaches T_review, otherwise clean. An AI-verified finding keeps its band even if a killed heuristic is what sent it to the AI: the AI judged the message itself, and the early-AI safety rule already sends its ban to admins. `Rails.FilterFinding` (no settings) is the same without the T_review check.
- Counters are in memory and seeded from group_bans with `Rails.NoteBans` at start-up.
