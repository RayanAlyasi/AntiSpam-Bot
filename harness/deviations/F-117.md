# F-117 deviations

- **Loose thresholds 12 bits / Jaccard 0.4** (the architect's assumption; DESIGN gives none). A
  loose match only routes to the AI or admins.
- **Matching key:** fingerprints are computed on the lower-cased TR39 skeleton of the normalized
  text (`neardup.Key`), so homoglyph and case swaps match. The corpus index recomputes this key
  from each entry's stored normalized text instead of using wp-g's stored fingerprints (raw
  `Norm`, 4-character shingles), so the stored and live sides always compare the same way.
- **Lookup is a linear scan** over the group's and the global index. v0.1 corpora hold thousands
  of entries at most, well inside the T1 budget. wp-g's LSH indexes can replace it without
  changing results.
- **Surfaces shorter than 8 runes** aren't matched, so a short reply can't be a near-duplicate of
  a confirmed message.
- **Only active and promoted entries match;** provisional ones are never hard evidence (§7.5).
- **The corpus reloads every 30 s** and right after a confirmation or overturn in this process:
  corpus_entries changes send no NOTIFY.
- **Whole-message matching (production):** confirmed entries store redacted text (the spam's
  numbers become keyed-hash tokens; emails and mentions are masked). The analyzer therefore also
  matches the live message's text built the same way (`corpus.FromMessage`, via
  `WithWholeText`), so a verbatim resend with a number or an email is certain.
- **Entries under 4 distinct tokens** can only match loosely, never CERTAIN.
- **Allow-lists are checked across every index** that serves the group (its own and the global
  one) before any match.
- **One allow-listed form suppresses the whole message's near-duplicate match:** if any surface or
  the whole text is within the CERTAIN limits of an overturned fingerprint, the analyzer matches
  nothing for that message. That way an overturned message with a phone number can't come back
  through its unredacted surface. Trade-off: someone who knows an overturned text could attach it
  as a second surface to hide a near-copy from this analyzer. The rule, blacklist, domain and
  exact-hash analyzers still run on the message.
