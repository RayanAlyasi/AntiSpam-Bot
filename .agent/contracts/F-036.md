# F-036: Leetspeak variant, separator collapsing, repeat squeeze, spam emoji

> Acceptance tests were waived by the user's fast-track decision for v0.1 work package A (this feature isn't core). The builder's table, property and fuzz tests carry the proof, and the judge checks them against this contract.

**Goal:** `Norm` collapses letter-by-letter spelling and squeezes stretched letters. A separate `Leet` variant folds digits and symbols used as letters. Common spam emoji become feature tokens. `Raw` keeps everything.

**Behaviour**
- **Separator collapsing (in `Norm`):** a run of 3 or more single letters (a letter plus its marks) separated by short gaps of spaces and/or `. - _ * · • , | / + ~` collapses: `f.r.e.e`, `f r e e`, `f-r-e-e`, `f . r . e . e` and spaced Arabic letters (`ر ب ح`) give the `Norm` of `free`/`ربح`. Digits never collapse (`1 2 3`, `1.2.2026`, `055 123 4567`, `10.0.0.1` stay as they are). A run that a separator joins to a longer word isn't collapsed, so hostnames (`a.b.c.com`) and initials before a surname stay. Runs of 2 (`a b`, `a.m`) stay.
- **Repeat squeeze (in `Norm`):** 3 or more identical letters in a row become 2: `freeeee`→`free`, `cooool`→`cool`, `ههههه`→`هه`. Runs of 2 (`good`) and digits (`1000000`, `0555`) are unchanged.
- **Leet (`Leet` field):** derived from `Norm`, folding at least `0→o 1→i 3→e 4→a 5→s $→s @→a`, then collapsing and squeezing again. `fr33`, `m0ney`, `cr4zy`, `$ale`, `c@sh`, `fr1end` and `f.r.3.3` give a `Leet` equal (EqualFold) to that of the plain word. Folding happens only inside a word (a maximal run of letters and leet characters) that has at least as many letters as leet characters, so `0555123456`, `$100`, `US$100`, `x100`, `2026` and `+966` are unchanged in `Leet`. `Norm` never gets leet folds. When nothing folds, `Leet == Norm`.
- **Spam emoji (`EmojiTokens`):** each occurrence of an emoji from a builder-maintained spam set adds one token, in order of appearance. Required at least: 💰 `money_bag`, 💸 `money_wings`, 🔥 `fire`, 📈 `chart_up`. Tokens are lowercase ASCII snake_case. Skin tones and variation selectors don't change the token. Other emoji (❤️, 👍🏽, 👨‍👩‍👧) give no tokens. Emoji stay in `Raw` and `Norm`. The slice is nil when there are none.
- F-034 properties of `Norm` still hold over the whole pipeline: NFKC-normal, idempotent (`Text(Norm).Norm == Norm`), no listed invisibles, linear time. **These stages never add to `Stripped`.** `buy crypto now` keeps its `Norm`.

**Public surface:** package `internal/normalize`: new fields `Result.Leet string` and `Result.EmojiTokens []string`, filled by `Text`. No new functions. Matchers prepare patterns with `Text(pattern)` and read the same field.

**Out of scope:** word-form obfuscation (`free` spelled `eff ar ee`), squeezing punctuation or emoji, scoring weights for emoji tokens, case folding (matchers compare case-insensitively).

**DESIGN.md refs:** §7.2 steps 4, 6, 8.

**Assumptions**
- Collapse and squeeze go in `Norm` because §7.2 lists them as `Norm` steps. They're limited to letters, so numbers and phone numbers survive for F-038. "Squeeze" keeps 2, not 1, so English double letters (`free`, `good`) survive.
- §7.2 offers `1→i/l`. `Leet` uses `i`, and `Skeleton` (F-035, TR39 `1→l`) covers the `l` reading.

**Proof (builder tests):** table tests for every example above (positive and unchanged), including the number and hostname non-cases; `Stripped` 0 for all of them; emoji token order and counts; a fuzz target extending F-034's properties to the new pipeline.
