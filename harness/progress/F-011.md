## 2026-10-17 · F-011 · per-bot connector lease in Postgres (WP-F, PR 2)

- Built: `bots.lease_owner`/`lease_until`, with `store.Bots.Acquire` (free, expired or ours), `Renew` (only while ours and unexpired) and `Release` (only ours), all judged by the database clock. `connector.Manager` renews every 10 s with a 30 s TTL and disconnects at once when a renew fails or the lease is gone. The lease owner ID is host/PID/random, so a restarted process never adopts an old lease. `antispam pair` takes the lease too while it sets the profile.
- Tests: `TestBotLease` (integration: exclusivity, expiry takeover, stale renew refused, release only by the owner), `TestOnlyOneProcessConnectsABot`, `TestLostLeaseDisconnects`.
