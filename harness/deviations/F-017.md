## 2026-10-17 · F-017 · §16, §10.5

- **New package `internal/alert`** (not in the §4 list): alerts and health are shared by the connector, the executor and the API.
- **Alerts are deduplicated per key for 30 minutes.** DESIGN.md says "deduplicated" without a window.
- **The `bot` metric label carries the masked number** (`966•••••••78@s.whatsapp.net`), not the full phone number, since metrics may be scraped by a hosted Grafana.
- **The email sink can't be cancelled** (net/smtp has no context support). Delivery runs off the hot path, so a slow server only delays later alerts.
