## 2026-10-17 · F-064 · actions table and transactional enqueue (WP-H)

- Built: migration `00004_actions.sql` (`app.actions` with kind, payload, priority P0–P4, status,
  attempts, not_before, deadline, message_time, unique dedupe_key, reason_detection_id, result;
  `app.action_controls` for the kill switch, breaker, ceiling and slowed rate), sqlc queries in
  `internal/store/queries/actions.sql`, and `actions.PGStore` implementing `actions.Store`.
  `PGStore.EnqueueTx(ctx, tx, action)` enqueues in the caller's transaction, so an action commits
  or rolls back with the detection that caused it. Operator changes and their audit_log rows commit
  together (`AuditedControl`); `PGAuditor` writes audit entries; `ConfigFromPacing` maps
  config.yaml `pacing` onto the executor.
- Contract: `.agent/contracts/F-064.md` (Postgres section). Deviations: `harness/deviations/F-064.md`.
- Also fixed the judge's minors from the first WP-H PR (#22): yields don't use up attempts, the
  deadline is re-checked after waiting for the call lock, batches yield when the bot bucket is empty.

### Follow-ups
- `reason_detection_id` has no foreign key yet; add one when the detections table exists.
- Finished sends keep their payload text and image (a review post may quote a message): the
  pruning job should clear or delete them within the §14 retention (DESIGN.md §17, §18).
- Retention of finished actions (the dedupe key is unique forever, so old rows are kept) needs a
  pruning job; keys then stay unique only for the retention period.
- `WritesSince` counts actions that reached WhatsApp, not calls (a batch of 8 kicks counts 8).
