# F-125 deviations

- §10.3 read budget: the blacklist's GetUserInfo lookups are paced by their own interval (one batched IQ every 2 s, `cmd/antispam/blacklist.go`) rather than through the executor's read bucket, which the chat process doesn't hold. They only run on `.blacklist add`, at most 50 numbers per IQ, far below the read budget; a process without a WhatsApp connection skips resolution and the entry still works as a content signal.
