## 2026-10-17 · F-022 · merge, back-fill and recycled numbers (WP-F, PR 1)

- Built: learning a pair for a PN-only person and a separate LID person merges the PN-only record into the LID person (`Tx.Merge`: identities and memberships move; memberships of a shared group are combined: earliest first-seen and join, summed counts, highest role); the merge is recorded and `Events.Resolved` fires with `MergedFrom` (the back-fill trigger). A known PN seen with a different LID closes the old link (`ValidTo`), links the PN to the new LID's person, never merges, raises `Events.RecycledNumber` for the operator, and emits no `Resolved`, so number-based bans can't follow the number.
- Deferred to the follow-up PR: the Postgres merge, which re-points every `app.*` column with a foreign key to `app.persons(id)` (strikes, detections, bans, corpus… as other packages add them), `person_merges` and the audit-log row (F-006); wiring `Events` to a River back-fill job.
- Follow-ups: events are emitted after the write commits, so a crash in between loses the event; the Postgres version should enqueue the back-fill job in the same transaction (River `InsertTx`).
