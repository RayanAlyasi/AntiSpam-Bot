# F-037: Arabic and Persian normalization

> Acceptance tests were waived by the user's fast-track decision for v0.1 work package A (this feature isn't core). The builder's table, property and fuzz tests carry the proof, and the judge checks them against this contract.

**Goal:** Arabic and Persian spellings of the same word give the same `Norm`, and a `Loose` variant also merges the letter pairs that are commonly swapped in informal writing.

**Behaviour**
- **In `Norm`:**
  - tashkeel is removed (U+064B–U+065F, U+0670, and the Quranic marks U+06D6–U+06ED), as is tatweel (U+0640);
  - alef forms unify (أ إ آ ٱ → ا);
  - Persian ک→ك and ی→ي;
  - Arabic-Indic (U+0660–U+0669) and Persian (U+06F0–U+06F9) digits become ASCII `0`–`9`.

  So `مَرْحَبًا`, `مرحـــبا` and `مرحبا` share a `Norm`. So do `أحمد`/`احمد`, `کتاب`/`كتاب`, and `٠٥٥١٢٣`/`۰۵۵۱۲۳`/`055123`. Decomposed hamza forms give the same result as precomposed ones.
- **`Loose` field:** `Norm` plus ى→ي and ة→ه (the "match variant" of §7.2). `مدرسة`/`مدرسه` and `على`/`علي` share a `Loose` but keep distinct `Norm`s. When nothing folds, `Loose == Norm`.
- Tashkeel and tatweel removal is folding, not obfuscation: **`Stripped` doesn't change** (Arabic with full tashkeel gives `Stripped == 0`, as in F-034). Arabic-script combining floods are still counted by F-034's rule.
- Non-Arabic text is unaffected: Latin, Hebrew, Devanagari and Urdu text without the listed characters keeps its `Norm`. F-034 properties (NFKC-normal, idempotent, linear) hold over the whole pipeline, and the order with F-036 makes `ر ب ح`, `رِبْح` and `ربـــح` all collapse to `ربح`.

**Public surface:** package `internal/normalize`: new field `Result.Loose string`, filled by `Text`. No new functions.

**Out of scope:** hamza-on-carrier folds (ؤ→و, ئ→ي), Urdu-specific letters, Arabic spelled-out digits (F-038 reads those for phone numbers), transliteration (Arabizi such as `7abibi`), dialect handling.

**DESIGN.md refs:** §7.2 step 5.

**Assumptions**
- Only ى→ي and ة→ه are lossy enough to need the separate variant, because they merge distinct words (`على`/`علي`). Everything else in step 5 goes in `Norm`, as §7.2 lists it.
- Digits fold in `Norm` so phone extraction (F-038) and keyword lists see ASCII. Arabic-Indic digits in `Raw` stay as written for evidence.

**Proof (builder tests):** table tests for each fold (with/without tashkeel, tatweel, each alef form, Persian letters, both digit sets, both `Loose` pairs and their distinct `Norm`s); `Stripped` 0 on ordinary Arabic and Persian text; a fuzz target extending F-034's properties.
