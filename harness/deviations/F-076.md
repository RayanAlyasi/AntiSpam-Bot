# F-076 deviations (safety rails)

- §8.6 "beyond that the bot pauses and alerts": the deletion circuit stays open until an admin or the operator resets it (`Rails.ResetCircuit`); it does not close on its own. While open, decisions are recorded as shadow and nothing is enqueued.
- Bans over the hourly cap become review actions with a shared `ReviewRequest.Batch` key per group and cap window; the review layer (wp-k) merges requests with the same key into one "kick the remaining N?" item.
- Per-rule kill switches are applied by `Rails.FilterFinding` before Decide: killed signals and violations are removed; a finding that was CERTAIN only through killed known evidence drops to still uncertain (admins), one with no evidence left becomes clean. The score itself is not recomputed.
- Counters are in memory and seeded from group_bans with `Rails.NoteBans` at start-up.
