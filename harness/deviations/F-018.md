## 2026-10-17 · F-018, F-023 · §11, §8.1, §4

- **The 6-hourly refresh uses one `GetJoinedGroups` per bot** instead of one `GetGroupInfo` per group: one IQ returns every group with its participants. It also finds groups the bot joined while offline.
- **The refresh runs in the connector role, not as a River job**, because it needs the bot's WhatsApp client, which only the connector process has when roles are split (§15).
- **Members of a group first seen by the sync are pre-existing** (they were there before the bot), as on a join; members a refresh finds in an already-known group without a join event are not (they may have joined while the bot was offline).
- **A member who leaves is deleted from `members`**; a rejoin starts tenure again (the "just joined" signal applies, §8.2). History belongs to `membership_history`, a later feature.
