# F-089 deviations

- **Fixtures live in `internal/replay/testdata/`**, not `testdata/fixtures/replay/`: agents can't
  write under testdata/fixtures.
- **Stored-inbox replay** (`antispam replay -inbox -since 24h [-group G] [-weights W]`) re-runs
  inbox rows through today's rules, corpus and settings with the engine alone: nothing is written,
  and the safety rails are a fresh in-memory copy. That is what the bot would decide now, not a
  re-creation of what it did then (`-shadow` lists that).
- **CI runs the replay tests** as part of the normal test run: the simulator test is untagged, and
  the Postgres harness replay runs with the integration tag.
