## 2026-10-17 · F-028 · Envelope builder: vCard parser, contact cards and business contacts

Part of the F-024 bundle (one PR; see F-024.md and `harness/deviations/F-024.md`).

- Built: `ParseVCard` (tolerant: folding, groups, bare and quoted params, quoted-printable, escapes; bounded at 64 KiB and 256 properties; fuzzed). Contact and contacts-array messages become `ContactRef`s with phones, waid numbers, biz name and description and URLs; every vCard field becomes a `vcard_*` text surface; `IsSelfContact` from the message or a waid equal to the sender's number.
- Deferred: Document-embedded vCards: `DocumentMessage.ContactVcard` is only a flag, the card itself needs a media download (T2), so it isn't parsed yet.
- Follow-ups: None.
