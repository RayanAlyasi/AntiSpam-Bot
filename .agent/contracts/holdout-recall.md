# holdout-recall: related corpus wording plus contact channels (post-v0.1.0 fix)

**Goal:** A reworded "list of services + contact" ad from a known campaign goes to the AI or admins. Related wording (`corpus.related`, +2.5) and an offered contact channel (`contact.advertised`, +1.0) are each below T_review on their own. Together they reach it, and neither can delete or ban.

**Behaviour**
- `neardup` has a third level, **Related**, ordered None < Related < Loose < Certain. CERTAIN and Loose, the SimHash/MinHash values, `Compute`'s existing fields and the allow-list check don't change. Stored allow-list fingerprints keep working.
- **Match terms** (`Terms(norm)`): distinct, lower-cased word tokens of normalized text, with these removed: URLs (scheme, www, bare `domain.tld/...`), the placeholders `[phone]` `[phone:<id>]` `[email]` `[mention]` `[name]` (the id may stay as a term), digit-only tokens under 3 digits, and a closed Arabic and English list of function words and greetings (at least the list in the delegation brief, including `كل عام وانتم بخير`). Arabic leading clitics (وال بال فال كال لل ال, lone و) are dropped only when ≥ 3 letters remain. `والواجبات`, `الواجبات`, `بالواجبات`, `للواجبات` and `واجبات` give one term, and `ولد` stays whole.
- **Related**: on distinct match terms, Jaccard ≥ 0.2, or containment (shared ÷ smaller side) ≥ 0.4 with the smaller side having ≥ 6 terms. Defaults are in `DefaultThresholds()`. Zero Related fields mean Related is off, so a 4-field literal still validates and never yields Related. Validate rejects Related limits that are negative or above 1.
- Fingerprints from `FingerprintText`, the ones the analyzer builds from surfaces and the whole text, and corpus entries loaded from storage or import (rebuilt from their stored redacted normalized text, with no new column) all support Related.
- An allow-listed (overturned) message matches at no level. The analyzer emits at most one corpus signal (best level wins). `corpus.related` has weight 2.5, is not Known or Hard, and its evidence names the entry.
- `contact` (T0, no I/O) emits at most one `contact.advertised` per message (weight 1.0, not Known or Hard), with evidence that names what was offered and masks numbers. It fires for: envelope `Phones`; a digit run outside URLs and mentions that `redact.Text` would mask as `[phone]` (national numbers, spaced numbers, Arabic-Indic digits); `Contacts`; `Invites`; chat.whatsapp.com links; a `ChannelRef` whose Source isn't `forwarded`; whatsapp.com/channel links; wa.me, api.whatsapp.com/send, t.me and telegram.me links in text (fullwidth too) or in `URLs`. It doesn't fire for: ordinary links (edu sites, YouTube, Google Maps, zoom.us with a meeting id), channel forwards, @mentions, prices, dates, short numbers, or the bare words "whatsapp" and "telegram".
- `pipeline.AnalyzerSet.Analyzers()` includes `contact`. `scoring.DefaultWeights().Version` changes from `2026-10-17.1`.
- End to end with default weights (bias −3.0, T_review 0.60): related alone → clean (p ≈ 0.38). Contact alone → clean. Contact + mixed script + light invisible chars → clean (p 0.50). Related + contact → UNCERTAIN from rules (p ≈ 0.62): still_uncertain without AI, never deleted or banned. Ordinary sick-leave chat → clean even though it is Related. Near-duplicate and similar outcomes don't change.

**Public surface:** `neardup`: `Related` (String "related"), `CodeRelated`, `WeightRelated`, `Terms(string) []string`, `Thresholds.{RelatedMinJaccard, RelatedMinContainment float64; RelatedMinTerms int}`, `Match.Containment float64`, existing `NewIndex/Add/Allow/Match/FingerprintText/NewAnalyzer`. `contact`: `New[P]()`, `CodeAdvertised`, `Weight`. `pipeline.AnalyzerSet`, `pipeline.NewEngine/Evaluate/NormalizeTexts`, `scoring.DefaultWeights`, `corpusimport` / `pgimport.Run` recall. Stubs: `internal/detect/neardup/related_stub.go` and `internal/detect/contact/contact_stub.go`. Replace them, renumber `Related` and add the fields.

**Out of scope:** lowering the Loose or CERTAIN limits; DB or migration changes; AI tier; a per-group toggle; the F-151 eval breakdown; corpus-import clustering (still at the Loose band).

**DESIGN.md refs:** §7.0, §7.1 (T0/T1), §7.3, §7.5; rules DET-001, DET-002.

**Assumptions:** "drop one clitic" conflicts with `واجبات` (it starts with و) equalling `الواجبات`. Only the equalities and the 3-letter guard are tested. Evidence masks phone numbers (GO-003, like the blacklist evidence). Digits inside URLs (zoom meeting ids) are not a phone even though the redactor masks them. A preview URL in `URLs` counts like a text link. The engine tests use established senders, so no new-member signal is involved.
