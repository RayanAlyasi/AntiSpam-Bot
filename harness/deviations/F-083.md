# F-083 deviations

- §13.7 `.ban` without a reason: the ban (kick + rejoin block) happens at once with reason `other` and the admin is asked; a `spam` DM within 10 minutes enqueues a second `Ban{spam}` and confirms the replied message as spam. Delaying the kick while waiting would leave a spammer in the group.
- §13.7 `.undo`: reverses bans/kicks (unban, never re-add) and strikes (trust restored) from the action-history port; other kinds are reported as nothing to undo.
